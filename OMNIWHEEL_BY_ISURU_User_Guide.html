🚀 Quick Start — How to Use OMNIWHEEL in 3 Easy Steps

1Select Any File

Click on a photo, document, video, or song in File Explorer or on your Desktop.

2Hold the Hotkey

Hold down ` (Tilde) or Alt. The circular conversion wheel appears instantly at your mouse!

3Point & Release

Move your mouse toward the format you want and let go of the key. That's it! Conversion is done.

Figure 1.1: The Front Wheel — Primary Formats Around Your Cursor

💡 What Makes OMNIWHEEL Special?

• Zero Waiting: No need to launch heavy third-party software just to convert a picture or document.

• Safe & Private: All conversions run directly on your computer. None of your private photos or documents are ever uploaded to the internet.

• Cancel Anytime: If you change your mind, simply move your mouse into the pink circle in the center (labeled CANCEL) and let go. Nothing will be touched!

• Multiple Files at Once: Select multiple files and convert or combine them in a single mouse gesture.

🎯 Helpful Pointer Tip

You do not need to click! Just hold the key, slide your mouse toward the slice, and release the key. The highlighted slice turns vibrant purple with a curved glow to confirm your choice.

👀 Page 4: Instant Spacebar Preview (Peek Inside Files)

Tired of waiting for heavy programs to open just to check what a file contains? OMNIWHEEL gives Windows the instant spacebar peek you've always wanted.

Figure 3.1: Instant Spacebar Peek • Press Left/Right Arrows to Browse

⚡ How It Works

  1. Click any file in Windows File Explorer.
  2. Tap Spacebar once. An instant window floats above everything in less than 50 milliseconds!
  3. Press ← or → arrow keys on your keyboard to flip through the rest of the files in your folder.
  4. Tap Spacebar or Esc to dismiss.

📁 What Files Can You Preview?

  • Photos: JPG, PNG, WEBP, GIF, SVG, BMP, TIFF, ICO.
  • Documents: PDF, Word (DOCX), Text (TXT), Markdown (MD).
  • Data: Spreadsheets (XLSX, CSV, TSV) in a readable table.
  • Music & Video: MP4, WebM, MP3, WAV with instant playback.
  • ZIP Folders: View inside ZIP archives without unzipping.
